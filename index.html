Files are parsed locally. Common identity fields are redacted; inspect exports for identifiers the parser may miss. Absence from a later scan does not prove a fault was repaired.

Supported first-version inputs

Text: VCDS-style Address headers, numeric “code - description” lines, and standard five-character P/B/C/U codes. JSON: an array or an object with a faults array, with module, code, and description fields. Entries are keyed by module and code; duplicate occurrences are collapsed. Unknown log layouts may produce an incomplete comparison.
